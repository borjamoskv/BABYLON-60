### Concept Manifesto: CORE VECTOR - ARTE_PURO | OUTPUT BIAS - POLISH_OVER_NOVELTY | FORBIDDEN MOVE - NONE

#### I. Introduction
Welcome to **CORE VECTOR - ARTE_PURO**, a conceptual framework designed to harmonize the essence of creativity with meticulous refinement. Our goal is to distill ideas into their most purest form, transcending initial novelty and emphasizing polished execution. This manifesto guides the creation of raw artifacts that resonate deeply with both simplicity and sophistication.

#### II. Core Vector - ARTE_PURO
- **Definition**: "ARTE_PURO" signifies a focus on artistry and the inherent beauty of simplicity. It is about capturing essence, stripping away excess, and elevating the core message.
  
- **Purpose**: To create artifacts that are not merely innovative but also timeless in their design and function.

#### III. Output Bias - Polish_Over_No Novelty
- **Philosophy**: Our primary focus shifts from novel ideas to polished execution. Novelty is celebrated; however, it must be complemented by refined craftsmanship.
  
- **Principles**:
  - **Simplicity**: Pursue clarity and minimalism in design, focusing on what truly matters.
  - **Quality**: Embrace the art of fine-tuning and refinement to ensure that every aspect of the artifact is flawless.
  - **Perfection**: Strive for excellence over mere innovation. Each artifact should be a testament to the highest standards of quality.

#### IV. Forbidden Move - None
- **Commitment**: We commit to not limiting creativity in any way. Novelty, complexity, and uniqueness are encouraged within the framework of our principles.
  
- **Enabling Creativity**: The absence of restrictions allows for unbridled exploration, fostering a rich tapestry of ideas that can be honed through the lens of polish.

#### V. Implementation
1. **Initial Idea Generation**:
   - Brainstorm novel ideas without immediate judgment.
   - Capture these ideas in raw form to preserve their originality and potential.

2. **Simplification**:
   - Strip away unnecessary elements, focusing on the core message.
   - Question each component of the idea to ensure its relevance and necessity.

3. **Polishing**:
   - Refine the simplified concept through meticulous attention to detail.
   - Consider feedback from others to gain additional perspectives and insights.
   - Iterate until the artifact is polished to a high degree, ensuring it meets or exceeds all standards of quality.

4. **Final Review**:
   - Conduct a final review to confirm that the artifact aligns with the principles of ARTE_PURO.
   - Ensure that each element contributes to the overall integrity and elegance of the final product.

#### VI. Conclusion
**CORE VECTOR - ARTE_PURO | OUTPUT BIAS - POLISH_OVER_NOVELTY | FORBIDDEN MOVE - NONE** offers a creative framework where simplicity and quality reign supreme. It encourages the exploration of novel ideas while emphasizing the importance of polished execution. By adhering to these principles, we strive to create raw artifacts that are both innovative and timeless in their design and function.

This manifesto is a call to action for creatives everywhere, inviting them to embrace the art of polish and refine their work with the same dedication they bring to novel ideas. Together, let us elevate the essence of creativity through the lens of ARTE_PURO.
