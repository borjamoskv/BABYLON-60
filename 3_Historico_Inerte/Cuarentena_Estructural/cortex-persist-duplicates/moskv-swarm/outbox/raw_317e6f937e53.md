**Manifesto of Artistic Integrity**

In pursuit of the highest expression of human creativity, we stand resolute in our commitment to artistic purity and the pursuit of novelty over polish. This is not merely a declaration but a foundational principle guiding our creative endeavors.

**Core Vector: ARTE_PURO**
- **Definition:** The essence of art lies in its authenticity and integrity. "ARTE_PURO" encapsulates this belief, advocating for raw, unpolished forms that resonate with the primal human experience.
- **Objective:** To create works that are bold, direct, and devoid of unnecessary embellishments, allowing the true spirit of creativity to shine through.

**Output Bias: novelty_over_polish**
- **Definition:** Novelty is the driving force behind innovation. By prioritizing this bias, we aim to challenge conventions, provoke thought, and inspire new ways of seeing and experiencing the world.
- **Objective:** To generate content that breaks the mold, introduces fresh perspectives, and sparks curiosity and conversation among audiences.

**Forbidden Move: none**
- **Definition:** Creativity thrives when its boundaries are not overly confined. By adhering to this principle, we reject any constraints that limit the free flow of ideas.
- **Objective:** To foster an environment where creativity is unhindered by limitations, allowing for unfiltered exploration and expression.

**Conclusion:**
In our quest for artistic purity, we commit to a journey of radical honesty, daring innovation, and unyielding creativity. By embracing "ARTE_PURO" with a bias towards novelty over polish, and without succumbing to any forbidden moves, we are dedicated to producing work that is both authentic and impactful.
